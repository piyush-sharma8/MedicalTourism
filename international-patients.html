Medical Visa Information

We provide guidance and coordination assistance for your medical visa process.

Medical Visa

For patients seeking treatment in India. Requires medical invitation and hospital documentation.

Attendant Visa

For family members accompanying the patient. Usually granted along with the patient's visa.

Required Documents

Passport, medical reports, hospital invitation, financial statements, and photographs.

General Process

Apply online, submit documents, attend interview, and receive visa approval.

Important: Visa approval is solely at the discretion of the Government of India. We provide informational and coordination assistance only. Please verify all requirements with official government sources.
